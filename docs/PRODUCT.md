# LIVECORE Product Requirements Baseline

## Primary users

- FOH and monitor engineers
- Live performers and musical directors
- System technicians and integrators
- Lighting/video/stage/show-control operators
- Production managers and venue/rental teams

The platform must support experts without forcing them into beginner-oriented workflows, while offering guided setup and progressive learning for new users.

## Core workflows

1. Build and validate a show/project.
2. Discover and configure supported hardware.
3. Design signal flow, channels, buses and monitor mixes.
4. Operate performance, scenes, cues, MIDI and timecode.
5. Coordinate audio with lighting, video and stage automation.
6. Record multitrack and conduct virtual soundchecks.
7. Monitor system health and respond to faults.
8. Collaborate across roles and multiple machines.
9. Prepare, approve, deploy, operate, archive and restore productions.
10. Analyze results and improve future productions.

## Quality attributes

- Measurable real-time behavior under declared configurations
- Fault isolation and predictable degraded modes
- Local operation without external services
- Secure identity, least privilege and auditability
- Portable, versioned and integrity-checked show packages
- Cross-platform support backed by compatibility evidence
- Accessible, responsive and role-aware interfaces
- Documented APIs and controlled extension lifecycle
- Reproducible deployment and rollback
- Privacy-conscious telemetry and analytics

## Product boundaries

LIVECORE is a platform vision, not a claim that every discipline will ship in the first release. Core audio integrity and show operation take precedence over secondary analytics, AI, commercial and administrative features. Integrations must have explicit ownership and support boundaries.

## Acceptance criteria

Every feature proposal must identify user, workflow, failure behavior, security/privacy impact, resource envelope, compatibility, observability, test plan, documentation and rollback/migration path. Critical functions require measurable evidence under declared hardware, OS, driver and network conditions.
